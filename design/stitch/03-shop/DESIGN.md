## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an upscale yet affectionate aesthetic tailored for discerning pet parents in modern metropolitan India. It balances bespoke heritage luxury with high-spirited charm. The interface feels tactile, sunny, and indulgent—reminiscent of curated boutique patisseries and private members' clubs, reimagined for the bond between companions and their pets.

### Core Tenets
- **Warm Tactile Sophistication:** The interface avoids cold tech-minimalism, relying instead on warm ivory canvases, velvety deep plum typography, and lush golden touches.
- **Affectionate Geometry:** Shapes prioritize hyper-soft contours, generous pill buttons, circular reward crests, and subtle linear paw/heart motif flourishes that soften structured luxury layouts.
- **Elevated Editorial Restraint:** Rich hues are applied deliberately to spotlight premium care services, couture accessories, and member milestones without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette relies on chromatic warmth and regal depth, strictly avoiding cool greens and sharp violets.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#6e1e3b` - Dried Plum):** Serves as the signature anchor for dominant actions, primary hero headers, key navigation states, and high-impact membership cards.
- **Secondary (`#f9dd8a` - Golden Yellow):** Functions as the celebratory accent. Used across wavy ribbon breaks, loyalty paw tokens, active tier badges, and high-value metric callouts.
- **Supporting Accent (`#a5bde1` - Powder Blue):** Used sparingly as a calming secondary signal—wellness indicators, booking confirmations, and gentle ambient pills.
- **Canvas / Neutral (`#fbf0d9` - Warm Ivory):** The primary app background, delivering a warm parchment substrate that reduces glare and enriches contrast.
- **Typography & High-Contrast Neutral (`#3d1426` - Deep Plum Ink):** Replaces pure black to ensure extreme legibility while preserving chromatic harmony across body text, secondary labels, and detailed metadata.

## Typography

The typography couples the rounded, sculptural confidence of Outfit for titles with the clean, highly legible precision of Plus Jakarta Sans for reading and microcopy.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Rendered in Outfit with tight negative tracking to impart a bespoke editorial punch. Headers command space and pair with playful line illustrations.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers pristine readability at dense scale, set at medium to regular weights using Deep Plum Ink (`#3d1426`) for optimal optical comfort.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Rendered in bold cuts of Plus Jakarta Sans with slight positive letter spacing, used on circular status badges, grooming timeline chips, and pricing tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile-first presentation, utilizing cozy paddings and dynamic stacking to replicate high-end concierge interactions.

### Spatial Rules
- **Base Grid:** 4-column layout on mobile devices scaling to a fluid 8-column layout on tablets with persistent 1.25rem outer margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Screen sections use generous vertical cushions (`space-xl` / 2.5rem) to evoke an unhurried, spacious luxury catalog experience.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-md` (1rem) for condensed feeds and `space-lg` (1.5rem) for primary profile/concierge booking modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages warm physical layering and gentle ambient diffusion rather than harsh technical drops.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Ground level warm ivory (`#fbf0d9`).
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure soft white (`#ffffff`) or tint-layered cream containers floating with ambient, warm-tinted shadows: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(61, 20, 38, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Controls & Navigation):** Elevated navigation pills and active sheets cast a deeper, warm glow: `0 14px 32px -6px rgba(110, 30, 59, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** High-level overlay surfaces supported by a blurred backdrop wash (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) tinted with 20% Dried Plum opacity.
- **Surface Accents:** Micro-borders use a warm 1px hairline tint: `rgba(61, 20, 38, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a hyper-rounded, welcoming shape language. Elements lean into generous pill profiles and soft pebble geometries to convey warmth, safety, and indulgence.

### Shape Expressions
- **Standard Controls:** All primary interactive buttons, filter chips, and pill tabs utilize complete pill radii (`rounded-full`).
- **Content Cards & Containers:** Card surfaces use extra-large radii (`rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem - 2rem) to soften boundaries and create snug framing.
- **Micro Accents:** Avatar rings, reward tokens, and alert icons feature perfect geometric circles (`rounded-full`) complemented with delicate 1.5px organic line illustrations.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) Dried Plum (`#6e1e3b`) button with white typography (`#ffffff`), 16px vertical padding, and a gentle downward shadow. Pressed states shift slightly to Deep Plum Ink (`#3d1426`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Golden Yellow (`#f9dd8a`) pill button with Deep Plum Ink (`#3d1426`) typography for rewards and upsell actions.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Ivory or transparent fill with a 1.5px hairline border in `#6e1e3b` and label text in `#6e1e3b`.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill silhouettes. Inactive states feature Warm Ivory with subtle `#3d1426` borders; active states feature solid Dried Plum with crisp white text or Powder Blue (`#a5bde1`) with Deep Plum Ink text for wellness status.

### Cards & Surfaces
- Surfaces rendered in white or off-white with `rounded-3xl` corners, subtle 1px border (`rgba(61, 20, 38, 0.06)`), and low-density warm plum shadows.
- Hero member cards feature rich Dried Plum backgrounds, accented by delicate Golden Yellow wavy borders and watermarked line-art paw emblems.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Roomy, pill-soft fields (`rounded-2xl`) filled with crisp white against the ivory background. Active focus is indicated by a 2px stroke in Dried Plum and a subtle golden halo.
- Floating labels in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Reward Badges & Crests
- Circular Golden Yellow medallions featuring high-contrast plum icons, bordered with dashed or double-stroke decorative rings mimicking artisanal pet collar tags.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom circular indicators with 2px Dried Plum borders. Active selections fill with Dried Plum housing an ivory check or dot.
